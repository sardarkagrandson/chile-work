# Kick-off: bar pattern speeds in DESI dwarfs (Robin Thomas & Virginia Cuomo)

Date: October 2026 (first meeting after the e-mail exchange of 14-21 Oct 2026)

## Where this comes from

* Robin's DESI DR1 dwarf-galaxy paper (properties and dynamics of dwarfs) was
  submitted in October 2026. Robin proposed revisiting the 2025 FONDECYT
  postdoctoral project (barred dwarfs, environmental dependence).
* FONDECYT rules changed in 2026: applicants without a permanent Chilean visa
  are not admitted, so the postdoctoral application cannot simply be resubmitted.
  Virginia has no travel funds to invite Robin. Agreed strategy: build a common
  project remotely, produce preliminary results, and use them to justify
  travel/visit funding later.
* Virginia's proposal (e-mail, 21 Oct 2026): apply the Tremaine-Weinberg (TW)
  method to enlarge the sample of dwarf barred galaxies with measured bar
  pattern speed. Start with galaxies that have archival MUSE data. Virginia
  also has one extra galaxy with new ROSIE-IFU data (Magellan/IMACS image
  slicer). Meet regularly (weekly was suggested by Robin).

## Decisions taken in the meeting

1. Parent sample: DESI DR1 star-forming galaxies (BGS), restricted to dwarfs.
   Cross-check bars with the DESI (Legacy Imaging) barred-galaxy catalogue
   (`Properties_of_bars_for_galaxies_from_DESI_legacy_imaging_surveys.fits`,
   89,780 SGA galaxies; provided by Virginia).
2. Cross-match the barred dwarfs with the ESO archive for MUSE data to refine
   the sample to objects that can be analysed now.
3. Apply the TW method to the final sample to measure bar pattern speeds
   (and corotation radius / rotation rate R) of the barred dwarfs.

## Questions to settle next meeting

* Dwarf mass limit and whether to include low-mass S0/quenched dwarfs (TW works on
  stellar kinematics; star formation is not required for TW, it is a choice
  of parent sample). Suggestion: keep "star-forming" as the primary sample
  (matches Robin's paper and the SF-vs-bar science), but keep a secondary
  list of quiescent barred dwarfs with MUSE data for completeness.
* Circular velocity: stellar + asymmetric drift (as in the Virgo dwarf papers)
  vs. Halpha gas rotation (available for star-forming targets in the same MUSE
  cube). Plan: do both, use the difference as a systematic.
* Where to publish preliminary results (poster at a 2027 meeting? SOCHIAS?) and
  which funding calls to target with them (ANID "Fomento a la vinculación
  internacional", ESO/Chile visitor programme, Robin's home-institution travel
  funds, bilateral calls).
