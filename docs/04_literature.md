# Annotated literature (to be extended as we read)

## Method
* Tremaine & Weinberg 1984, ApJ 282, L5 -- the method.
* Debattista 2003, MNRAS 342, 1194 -- PA errors as the dominant TW systematic.
* Corsini 2011, Mem. SAIt Suppl. 18, 23 -- review of TW results.
* Cuomo et al. 2020, A&A 641, A111 -- review/compilation of TW pattern speeds; ultrafast bars discussion.
* Zou et al. 2019, ApJ 884, 23 -- TW tested on simulations; slit length and PA effects.
* Williams et al. 2021, AJ 161, 185 -- TW on PHANGS-MUSE with stellar mass maps; quality criteria.
* Krajnovic et al. 2006, MNRAS 366, 787 -- kinematic PA (pafit).
* Cappellari 2017, 2023 -- pPXF; Cappellari & Copin 2003 -- Voronoi binning.

## Large TW samples (massive galaxies)
* Aguerri et al. 2015, A&A 576, A102 -- CALIFA, 15 bars; asymmetric drift V_c.
* Guo et al. 2019, MNRAS 482, 1733 -- MaNGA, 53 bars.
* Garma-Oehmichen et al. 2020, MNRAS 491, 3655 -- MaNGA/CALIFA; PA error; kinematic vs photometric PA.
* Garma-Oehmichen et al. 2022, MNRAS 517, 5660 -- 97 Milky Way analogues; restricting i and dPA halves the ultrafast fraction.
* Geron et al. 2023, MNRAS 521, 1775 -- 225 MaNGA bars; strong bars slower; public code.
* Aguerri et al. 2025 -- critique of TW error budgets in large samples.

## Dwarfs
* Cuomo et al. 2022, MNRAS 516, L24 -- IC 3167: slow lopsided bar in an infalling Virgo dwarf (R = 1.7 +0.5/-0.3).
* Cuomo et al. 2024, MNRAS 527, 11218 -- NGC 4483 and NGC 4516: short, weak, slowish bars (NGC 4516: Omega_p = 24.6 +/- 0.8 km/s/kpc, R = 1.75); comparison with massive bars.
* Cuomo et al. 2019, A&A 632, A51 -- NGC 4264 with MUSE (methodology template for MUSE TW).
* Sanchez-Janssen et al. 2010 -- thickness of dwarf discs (q0).
* Debattista & Sellwood 2000; Athanassoula 2003 -- dark matter braking -> slow bars.

## Data
* DESI Collaboration 2025 (DR1, arXiv:2503.14745); Moustakas et al. (FastSpecFit, Iron VAC).
* Walmsley et al. 2023, MNRAS 526, 4768 -- Galaxy Zoo DESI.
* Moustakas et al. 2023, ApJS 269, 3 -- Siena Galaxy Atlas 2020.
* "Properties of bars for galaxies from DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys" -- SGA-based bar catalogue (reference to be added once the authors/paper are confirmed).
* McGurk et al. 2020, SPIE -- ROSIE IFU on Magellan/IMACS (50"x54" field, R ~ 2000, 1800 A coverage).
* 2026 DESI bar papers: "Investigating the effects of bars on SF and nuclear activity" (arXiv:2605.04537); "Living with Neighbors VI" (arXiv:2510.18937) -- GZ DESI bar selection conventions.
